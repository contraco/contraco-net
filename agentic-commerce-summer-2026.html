The Loud Summer of 2026: A Chronicle

From Alipay to SAP Sapphire: a compact, dated chronicle of the agentic commerce announcements that changed the market in 2026. A reference piece for the full cluster.

By contraco | August 2026

A Reference Chronicle

The agentic commerce infrastructure that reached production scale in 2026 was built across a compressed period of announcements, protocol launches, and ecosystem expansions. This chronicle documents the key events in order. It is a reference for the cluster of analyses linked at the end, not a comprehensive market survey.

February 2026
Alipay: 120M Transactions in a Single Week
Alipay reported more than 120 million AI Pay transactions in a single week, February 5 to 11, 2026, and describes AI Pay as the first agentic payment service to reach that milestone. [Q9] It operates in a single-operator context where identity, payment, and agent layer are controlled by one entity, which is not the European structural model, but it demonstrates what the infrastructure is capable of when integrated end to end.
March 2026
OpenAI: Agentic Commerce Protocol
OpenAI detailed how its Agentic Commerce Protocol supports product discovery, with Target, Sephora, Nordstrom, Lowe's, Best Buy, The Home Depot and Wayfair already integrated and Walmart launching its own app inside ChatGPT. [Q15]
May 2026
Amazon and Google I/O
Amazon reworked its shopping assistant into a purchasing agent capable of executing transactions on behalf of users. [Q16] At Google I/O, Google extended its Agent Payments Protocol (AP2), the signed-mandate framework first announced in September 2025, to let agents make purchases autonomously. [Q6] These two announcements in the same month signaled that the major commerce platforms were moving from conversational AI to transactional AI simultaneously.
June 2026
Mastercard Agent Pay for Machines
On June 10, Mastercard launched Agent Pay for Machines, extending its Agent Pay programme of April 2025 to programmatic payments between agents and machines across cards, bank accounts and stablecoins, with more than 30 launch partners. [Q3, Q4] Verifiable Intent, the tamper-resistant record of what a user authorised, had been announced with Google on March 5. [Q18]
July 2026
Visa Europe: Live at Scale
Visa Intelligent Commerce went live in Europe with more than 30 issuer partners, and with merchants including lastminute.com, Frasers, Cleverbridge and BrickDepot. [Q1] The Trusted Agent Protocol and Agent Directory moved from announced infrastructure to production infrastructure. Separately, Ant International closed a Series A financing of about USD 1.2 billion to expand its cross-border payments and AI-enabled commerce business. [Q17]
Ongoing: SAP
SAP AI Agent Hub: Sapphire 2026
At Sapphire 2026, SAP opened its AI Agent Hub, a vendor-agnostic system of record to inventory and govern AI agents across an enterprise, to more customers. [Q7, Q8] The Hub addresses the back-office end of the control layer: the point at which an agent-executed purchase must be reconciled, approved, and recorded within an enterprise's governance infrastructure.

What This Chronicle Shows

Between February and August 2026, both major card networks, OpenAI, Amazon, Google, SAP and Alipay each announced or released agentic commerce infrastructure. This is not a coincidence of timing. It reflects a shared assessment across organizations with competitive interests that the infrastructure readiness threshold had been reached.
