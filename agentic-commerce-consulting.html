Agentic Commerce Consulting

Your customers will stop comparing options themselves. Software will do it for them, inside a frame they set beforehand. contraco helps companies assess that shift and make the decisions that cannot be corrected cheaply once volume arrives.

What we do

We advise companies on agentic commerce, from first assessment through to a defensible implementation decision. That covers four questions which belong together and are almost always handled separately. What level of autonomy your customers buy at today and in twelve months. Who a purchase is attributed to when an agent triggers it inside a frame configured in advance. Which of your product data a machine can actually evaluate. And who inside the company owns the answer when the technology moves again.

Four areas of work

Establish maturity

Six levels describe how autonomously buying happens in your market. Where you sit determines which obligations already apply and which do not. Companies that have not established their level reliably invest in the wrong one.

Resolve attribution

A software agent is not acting on its own account. The purchase is attributed to the person who configured it, and only as far as it stayed inside the frame they set. That frame has to be reconstructible after the fact, which makes it a product and logging requirement before it is a legal one.

Make data agent readable

An agent evaluates fields, not photography and prose. Offers whose conditions exist only in narrative are not rejected, they are never considered. We establish what a machine can actually see of your catalogue.

Governance and evidence

Regulation governs how a system may be operated. It does not answer who a declaration binds. Both need their own answer and both need someone accountable inside the company. We help place that accountability before a dispute goes looking for it.

Who we work with

We work with mid sized and large organisations in retail, travel, financial services and industry whose problem cannot be solved inside one department. If a question can be answered by IT, sales or legal alone, you do not need an outside firm and we will say so. When strategy, systems and organisation have to move together, that is the work.
